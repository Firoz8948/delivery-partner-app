Return, Cancellation and Refund Policy

Food orders are prepared specifically for the customer and are time-sensitive.

Orders cannot be cancelled or returned after placement

Once an order is confirmed on LalganjEats, preparation and delivery operations may begin immediately. Customers cannot ordinarily cancel, return or exchange the order because of a change of mind, an incorrect address entered by the customer, unavailability at delivery, refusal to accept delivery or preference/taste.

When a refund may be reviewed

A refund or adjustment may be considered after verification where the restaurant cannot fulfil the order, payment is duplicated, an online payment fails but is captured, the complete order is not delivered, or applicable law requires a remedy. Wrong, missing, materially damaged or unsafe items should be reported immediately with photographs or other requested evidence.

Customer refusal and account action

If a valid order below ₹500 is deliberately refused or rejected, the incident may be investigated. An administrator may restrict, suspend or ban the customer account after review. The customer may contact support to explain exceptional circumstances.

Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method. Processing times depend on banks and payment providers. COD orders generally have no prepaid amount to refund; any approved adjustment may be provided through another supported method.
